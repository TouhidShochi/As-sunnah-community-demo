HELPFUL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

A preview of the information visitors could find on the finished website.

How do I enquire about Madrasah admission?

Use the Madrasah Admission demo to explore the proposed application journey. This demo does not submit a real application; contact the centre directly for current admissions.

Can I book a class or appointment online?

The booking journey on this concept website is interactive but simulated. A production website could connect to a live timetable and booking system.

Can I make a donation here?

No. The donation screen is a demonstration only and never processes payments. A future official website could integrate a secure payment provider with the centre's approval.

Is this the centre's official website?

No. This is an independent design proposal by Dreban Tech, created to demonstrate a possible future website.
